Overview

Sanah is a food and nutrition tracker. You photograph a meal, confirm or edit what the AI detected, and get a health score with a personalized explanation of why it scored that way.

It resolves nutrition against a dedicated Nigerian dish library first and falls back to USDA’s global database — accurate for Nigerian meals without being locked to them.

The problem

Nutrition data is often built around Western foods, which leaves Nigerian dishes poorly covered. And a calorie number on its own doesn’t tell you whether a meal was good for you or what to change.

My role

Sanah is a personal portfolio project that I build on my own.

What I owned

  • A four-stage AI pipeline: vision, tiered nutrition resolution, deterministic scoring, personalized explanation
  • Tiered nutrition lookup — West African Food Composition Table first, USDA underneath
  • Daily calorie and macro targets via Mifflin-St Jeor BMR/TDEE, tailored to the user’s goal
  • Real-time Firestore listeners for the calorie ring and recent meals

  1. 01

    A pipeline, not a single prompt

    Four stages, each with one job: vision extracts what’s on the plate; a tiered resolver matches it against a Nigerian dish library, falling back to USDA and then AI estimation; a deterministic scorer computes the health score; a final stage writes the explanation.

  2. 02

    Data that works beyond one region

    The West African Food Composition Table covers Nigerian dishes specifically, with USDA’s global database underneath for everything else — a tiered design rather than a single-market dataset with a fallback bolted on.

  3. 03

    Scores you can trust

    The health score is computed deterministically, so the same meal scores the same way every time. The model explains the score; it doesn’t invent it.

  4. 04

    Personal targets, live

    Onboarding computes daily calorie and macro targets with the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR/TDEE formula for the user’s goal, and Firestore listeners keep the calorie ring and recent meals in sync as entries are added or edited.
