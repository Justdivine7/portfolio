Overview

It sits on top of people’s money and orders, so it has to be dependable.

Paying a supplier in China, finding products and getting someone to source them usually means juggling several services. Midddleman puts the wallet, the currency purchase, the 1688 marketplace and the agent network in one app.

My role

Midddleman is a team product. I lead mobile development of the customer and agent apps, set technical direction and work with QA to validate builds. What follows is the part I personally own.

Engineering challenges

  1. 01

    Staying usable on unreliable networks

    I built the connectivity and offline layer that keeps the app usable instead of failing on every dropped request.

  2. 02

    Sessions and notifications

    Session-timeout handling for a wallet app, plus iOS push notifications and app-icon badging so updates reach people outside the app.

  3. 03

    Owning the release pipeline

    Release configuration and the pipeline across App Store Connect, TestFlight, Play Console and Shorebird OTA patches.
