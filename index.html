About

I care most about the parts of an app nobody screenshots — the architecture, the failure states, the release.

I’m a Flutter and Dart engineer based in Akure, Nigeria. Today I lead mobile development at Midddleman Technologies, where I own the release pipeline and the layers that keep the customer and agent apps dependable. Before that I was the sole mobile engineer at Jamforte Technologies, shipping Pawshauz and Difamer, and I’ve built delivery and sports apps on contract.

Alongside that I build AI-native products — Shmood and Sanah — to push on what a mobile app can do with voice, vision and language models, without letting a model make things up.

  1. 01

    Boundaries before features

    Clean Architecture, BLoC and dependency injection aren’t ceremony. When Shmood’s audio-layer swap surfaced an AVAudioSession conflict, those boundaries kept the fix inside one layer.

  2. 02

    Real networks are the main path

    Offline handling, caching and session management decide whether an app is usable for people on unreliable connections. I treat them as core features, not edge cases.

  3. 03

    Every state gets designed

    Payments succeed, fail and get cancelled. Streams get interrupted. I build for all of it — Paystack, Flutterwave and Monnify flows with every outcome handled.

  4. 04

    Shipping is part of the job

    Signing, provisioning, TestFlight, Play Console and Shorebird OTA patches. An app isn’t finished until it’s in people’s hands — and can be fixed fast once it is.
